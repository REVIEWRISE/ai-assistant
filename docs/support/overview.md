# VyntRise Agent — platform overview

VyntRise Agent helps local businesses automate appointments, reviews, and phone conversations from one workspace.

## Workspaces

- Each customer account works inside an **organization** (workspace).
- Switch organizations from the header when you belong to more than one.
- Team members are invited and managed under **User Management**.

## Main product areas

- **Appointment Agent** — knowledge base, chatbot, bookings overview, and organization settings.
- **Review Response** — connect review providers and manage replies.
- **Voice Agent** — AI phone line (included on Pro Voice).
- **Subscription** — view your plan, billing status, and refunds.
- **Support** — ask for help about the platform; an AI assistant replies first, and the platform team can follow up.

## Getting started

1. Complete onboarding and choose a plan (or start a trial when offered).
2. Set up your organization profile.
3. Approve a knowledge base so the chatbot can answer visitors accurately.
4. Connect calendars or review providers as needed for your plan.
