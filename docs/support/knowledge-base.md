# Knowledge base and chatbot

## Knowledge base

- Open **Appointment Agent → Knowledge Base**.
- Add business facts (hours, services, policies, FAQs) by typing, importing, or crawling a website.
- The knowledge base must be **approved** before the chatbot (and related AI booking answers) should treat it as live ground truth.
- If knowledge is empty or not approved, booking and Q&A quality will be limited; the product may block online booking until this is fixed.

## Chatbot

- Configure appearance and behavior under **Appointment Agent → Configure Chatbot**.
- Embed the chatbot on your site with the provided embed snippet.
- Answers should come from your approved knowledge — the assistant should not invent prices or services that are not documented.

## Appointments overview

- **Overview** shows bookings captured by the chatbot and (when enabled) voice.
- Connect a staff calendar when you want reservations synced automatically; otherwise the team follows up manually.
