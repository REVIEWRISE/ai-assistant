# Billing and subscription

## Plans

Plans are shown on the landing page and during onboarding. Common tiers include:

- **Starter** — core appointment/chat features for smaller teams.
- **Growth** — higher limits and more team capacity.
- **Pro Voice** — includes the AI phone / Voice Agent feature and voice minutes.

Exact prices, included locations, seat limits, and voice minutes are defined on the live pricing / Subscription pages. Do not invent dollar amounts.

## Managing billing

- Workspace owners can open **Subscription** to see the current plan and status.
- Platform billing admins manage organizations, plans, and refunds under **Billing** in the admin menu.
- Failed or expired billing can lock product features until the subscription is restored.

## Refunds

- Customers can request a refund from Subscription when the product allows it.
- Billing admins review refund requests in **Billing → Refunds**.

## Trials

- New workspaces may receive a trial period depending on plan configuration.
- When a trial ends without a paid subscription, access may be limited until a plan is chosen.
