# Voice Agent

The Voice Agent is the AI phone receptionist for your business.

## Availability

- Voice calling is included on **Pro Voice** only.
- Starter and Growth do not include the AI phone agent. Upgrade to Pro Voice to enable it.

## Setup basics

1. Open **Voice Agent** in the sidebar (when your plan includes it).
2. Configure the agent greeting, knowledge, and Retell-related settings.
3. Add or assign an AI phone line.
4. Optionally forward your published business number to the AI line on no-answer so callers reach the assistant when you miss the call.

## Call forwarding

- Keep your public business number; set carrier “forward on no answer” to your AI line.
- The in-app forwarding guide shows carrier-specific dial codes and can SMS activation steps to a phone you control (with consent).

## Minutes

- Voice minutes are metered according to your plan’s included allowance.
- If minutes are exhausted, calling may pause until the period resets or the plan is upgraded — check Subscription / Voice Agent for the current balance messaging.
